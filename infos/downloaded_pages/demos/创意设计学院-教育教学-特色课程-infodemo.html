| X STUDIO

X-Studio跨学科实践工作室是创意设计学院的特色课程之一。该课程提供了一个突破学科壁垒的设计研究与创作平台， 鼓励学生跨专业、跨年级、跨学院自由组队，在同一个核心设 计议题下进行深入的探索和实践，目标是培养学生在未来发展 中的综合素养、协作能力和广阔视野，使学生在复杂多变的社 会环境中更具竞争优势。

X-Studio, the interdisciplinary studio practice, is one of the signa ture courses of the School of Design and Innovation. This course provides a platform for cross-disciplinary design research and creation, encouraging students to form teams across majors, grades, and schools to engage in in-depth exploration and prac tice under one design topic. The goal is to cultivate students' comprehensive qualities, collaboration skills, and broad perspectives for their future career development, enabling them to gain a competitive edge in the complex and ever-changing social environment.
