CUTL Students went to Valeo(Germany) for Enterprise Internship
来源:
作者:
点击数:
2025/05/07 21:37:56

In September 2024, our college successfully sent 3 undergraduate students to the Wemding and Kronach branches of Valeo (Germany) for a 5-month enterprise internship. During the internship, the students will work in technical positions at Valeo (Germany), be exposed to technical work related to autonomous driving, and receive guidance and assistance from enterprise mentors.
